Save a backup

Export your passport, event checkoffs, official stamps, captions, and photos into a backup file. Keep it somewhere safe. Large photo collections can create a large file.

For extra protection, save important photos to your camera roll too. Browser storage can be cleared by device settings or lost if you change phones.

Privacy, in plain language

  • No account or login is required.
  • Photos and passport progress are saved locally in this browser.
  • Basehor does not receive or see the photos saved here.
  • Anyone with access to this device/browser may be able to open this scrapbook.
  • Official QR stamps are for fun recognition, not secure proof of attendance.

Reset this passport

This permanently deletes local passport data and saved photos from this browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.
